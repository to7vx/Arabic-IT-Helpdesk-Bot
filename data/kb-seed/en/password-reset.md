---
title: How to reset your password
tags: [account, password, self-service]
---

If you've forgotten your password, you can reset it from the login page without contacting IT.

## Steps

1. Open the login page and click **Forgot password?**.
2. Enter the email address associated with your account.
3. Check your inbox for an email titled *"Reset your password"*. The link is valid for 30 minutes.
4. Choose a new password that meets the policy: at least 12 characters, mixing letters, numbers, and symbols.
5. Confirm the new password and log in.

## If you don't receive the email

* Check the Spam folder.
* Confirm you used the correct email address.
* If still missing after 5 minutes, submit a ticket with the subject *"Password reset email not delivered"*.

## If you're locked out

After 5 failed login attempts the account is temporarily locked. Wait 15 minutes, then try again, or ask an administrator to unlock the account.
